Rec Room was a video game and an online platform that you could play. It let people meet, chat and play games in spaces. It launched in 2016. Rec Room gave users a chance to create avatars and explore millions of rooms. These rooms were made by both the developers and community members.
Rec Room had built-in mini-games. Players could play paintball, laser tag and work together on quests. Rec Room supported -platform play so you could join using a VR headset, PC, console or mobile phone. Rec Room also gave players tools, like the Maker Pen so you could. Build your worlds.
Rec Room officially stopped running. Its servers shut down on June 1 2026. That was the end of a ten-year journey. Many people saw Rec Room as a hub for VR. Rec Room brought people together in ways.
